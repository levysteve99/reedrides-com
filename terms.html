Ride safely

Always follow the park's rules and the ride operators' instructions. Don't use your phone on a ride, or anywhere the park doesn't allow it, and secure it before you board. Your check-in can wait until you're back on the ground.

Ride data, wait times and Ask Reed

Ride stats, park details and Coaster Trivia questions come from public sources that we check, but they can be wrong or out of date. Wait times come from Queue-Times.com and may lag behind the park's own signs. Don't rely on Reed Rides for height requirements, safety information or ride availability; check with the park. If you spot a mistake, tell us and we'll fix it.

Ask Reed uses an AI model, and AI can make mistakes. Check anything important. Ask Reed has a weekly question limit, which we may change.
