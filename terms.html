Using Reed Rides

Reed Rides is a companion app for tracking the rides you've been on, earning badges and stamps, and coordinating with a Crew of people you ride with. By creating an account, you agree to use the app honestly, keep your account credentials to yourself, and not misuse features like Crew invitations or check-ins (for example, sending invitations to people who haven't asked for one, or falsifying ride check-ins). We may suspend or remove accounts that abuse the app or its messaging features.

The app is provided as-is while we're building it out. Features, badges, and ride data may change as we improve accuracy and add parks.

SMS / text messaging program: Reed Rides Crew Invites

Reed Rides crew leaders and managers can invite people they know to join their Crew by entering that person's mobile number in the app. Doing so sends the recipient a one-time text message with a link to accept the invitation, and may send a single follow-up reminder if the invitation hasn't been accepted. This program is transactional, not marketing: messages are only sent when an existing Reed Rides user chooses to invite a specific person by name and number.

  • Program name: Reed Rides Crew Invites
  • Message frequency: Varies. Typically one invitation message per invite sent, with at most one reminder message if the invite is not accepted. You will not receive recurring messages unless you accept an invite, create a Reed Rides account, and separately opt in to further communications within the app.
  • Message and data rates may apply. Carrier fees vary by plan and carrier; check with your mobile provider for details.
  • How to get help: Reply HELP to any message for support, or email reed@reedrides.com.
  • How to opt out: Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving further texts from this program. You may receive one final confirmation message after doing so.
  • Supported carriers: Reed Rides is not responsible for undelivered or delayed messages caused by your carrier or device.
