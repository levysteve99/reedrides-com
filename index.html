How it works

From first drop to full passport

Four steps, no spreadsheets. Reed Rides handles the tracking so you can focus on the ride.

1

Check in for real

Get near a ride and Reed Rides confirms you're really there, using a geofence plus a short dwell timer. It's a verified visit, not just an honor system.

2

Collect your stamp

Every coaster earns its own ride stamp in your Passport the first time you check in, so what you've ridden ends up as an actual record, not just a memory.

3

Earn badges

Track progress across ride type, manufacturer, milestones, geography, elements, and full Park Master runs.

4

Ride with your crew

See who's at the park, sweep a ride together, and compare stamps and milestones with the people you actually rode with.

FAQ

Good to know

When does Reed Rides launch?+

We're finishing the native iOS and Android build now. Drop your email in the Get Notified box and we'll send one message the moment both stores approve it, no need to keep checking back.

What counts as a "verified" ride?+

Reed Rides checks that you're within range of the ride and that you stayed for a short dwell window before it counts as a verified visit. It's not just a tap on your phone from across the park. A manual log is always there if your location isn't cooperating.

Is it just for coaster credit-counters?+

No. Crew is built for people who ride together, not just people chasing a number. Presence, Sweep, and Recap are all about the group, not the solo tally.

Can younger riders use it?+

Yes. Reed Rides has age-appropriate defaults built in. Social features like Crew open up at 13, and location sharing is scoped down for younger accounts.

Do I need cell service for a check-in to count?+

No, just a GPS fix. Spotty cell service at the park usually doesn't matter. If you're somewhere with no signal at all, log the ride manually and it still counts.

Can I add trips from before I started using the app?+

Yes. Log a Past Trip lets you record earlier visits after the fact, complete with a real recap and any badges those rides qualify you for.

What if my home park isn't in Reed Rides yet?+

Tell us. We're adding parks steadily, and which one comes next is mostly driven by what people actually ask for.

Is Reed Rides free?+

Tracking, badges, stamps, and Crew are free. We're planning an optional paid tier down the line for deeper AI features, nothing about the core passport is going behind a paywall.

Can I delete my account and my data?+

That's being built into Settings. Until it ships, email us and we'll take care of it directly.

Does Reed Rides track my location in the background?+

No. Location only gets checked when you open a ride's check-in flow, not continuously, and not while the app is closed.

Do I have to join a crew to use the app?+

No. Passport, badges, stamps, and Hall of Fame all work the same whether you ever join a crew or not. Crew is there if you want it.

Will Reed Rides work outside the US, or in other languages?+

That's on the roadmap: metric units and additional languages beyond English. If you want to see one sooner, tell us which.

If I undo a ride by mistake, does it mess up my badges?+

No, the opposite. Undoing a check-in updates your stats and badges immediately, so nothing stays counted after you've corrected it.

Who's actually behind Reed Rides?+

Reed Rides is built by Reed (yes, of Coaster Wars) and family, not a big theme park company. If something's broken or missing, tell us. We're the ones who'll actually fix it.
