Built to stay in its lane.

  • Reads, never writes. Ask Reed can look things up. It can't check you in, undo a ride or change anything.
  • Only what you can already see. It uses the same permissions as the app: your data and your crew's shared stats, nothing more.
  • Says when it doesn't know. If a ride's speed or length was never published, it tells you instead of guessing.
  • 13 and up. Ask Reed isn't available on under-13 accounts, and younger crew members' rides are left out of crew comparisons.
  • Conversations aren't saved. Close the chat and it's gone.
  • 25 questions a week, free. The count resets every Monday.
Seat Collector

Front row, back row, every row.

When you check in, tap the seat you sat in on a map of that ride's actual train. Reed Rides keeps every seat, ride by ride, so a second ride in a new row counts too.

  • Seat maps for 500+ coasters, built from each ride's published train layout. Rides without one fall back to a simple row picker.
  • Front Row Club, Back Row Club and Seat Master: milestones for the riders who care where they sit.
  • Tag the seat: Airtime, Intense, Rough or Smooth. Once enough riders tag a ride, a heatmap shows where each one lands.
  • Fill the Train: see how much of the train your crew has covered between you, and who got each seat first.
In line

The wait is part of the ride.

Tap "I'm in line" and Reed Rides times your wait. Check in after the ride and it tells you how it went.

  • Line timer: starts when you get in line, stops when you check in, with the posted wait alongside where the park publishes one.
  • First ride? Call it first. Guess how scary it'll be before you board, then say how scary it really was. Your Passport keeps score.
  • "You sat next to Jordan!" Log the seat beside a crewmate and Reed Rides notices you rode side by side.
  • Trivia while you wait: a quick quiz about the park you're standing in.
  • Front Row Tax: how much longer the front row really takes. It appears once enough riders have timed a ride.

Nobody rides alone

The best part of a coaster trip is who you rode it with.

Crew is where Reed Rides comes alive. See who's at the park, sweep the rides as a group, fill a train together and get a recap of what everyone rode.

  • Crew Presence: who's at the park today and who's riding right now. It's off until you turn it on, and it never shows a map pin.
  • Crew check-in alerts: a notification when a crewmate with Crew Presence on checks in at a park.
  • Crew Sweep: unlock every coaster at a park together.
  • Seatmates and Fill the Train: who you've ridden side by side with most, and how much of each train your crew has covered.
  • Leaderboards: coasters ridden or milestones achieved. Rather not compete? Switch your view from rankings to shared progress.
  • Crew Recap: today, yesterday, the last 7 days, this month, this year or all time, with a member-by-member comparison.
  • More than one crew: family, park friends and a one-off trip, each with its own scores.

FAQ

Good to know.

When does Reed Rides launch?

We're finishing the iOS and Android apps now. Drop your email above and we'll send one message once it's live in both the App Store and Google Play.

What is Ask Reed?

An assistant inside the app that answers questions about your rides, seats, trips, badges and crew, plus facts about 650+ coasters. It reads your data; it can't change it. Everyone 13 and up gets 25 questions a week.

What counts as a "verified" ride?

Check In unlocks once you're within range of the ride and stay there for a short dwell timer. It's not a tap from across the park. If your location isn't cooperating, you can still log the ride by hand.

Can younger riders use it?

Yes. Everyone gives a birthday at sign-up, and it's never shown to anyone. Under-13 accounts get the passport, seats, badges, stamps and Coaster Trivia, and can join a crew after a parent or guardian confirms by email. Ask Reed is for 13 and up.

Is Reed Rides free?

Yes. Tracking, seats, badges, stamps, trivia, Crew and 25 Ask Reed questions a week are free, and there are no ads. We may add an optional paid tier for extras later, but the core passport stays free.

How do I sign in?

With a code or link we email you, Sign in with Apple on iPhone, or Google on Android. There's no password to remember.

Where do the wait times come from?

Queue-Times.com, refreshed every few minutes, at 65+ parks. Parks they don't cover simply don't show a wait, rather than a guess.

Can I add trips from before I started using the app?

Yes. Log a Past Trip records earlier visits, with a recap and any badges those rides earn.

Does Reed Rides track my location in the background?

No. Location is only checked while the app is open: for check-ins, distances to parks and the nearby-park prompt. The check happens on your phone, and we don't store your GPS coordinates.

What can my crew see?

Your ride history (which rides, when, and which seats), stats, recaps and leaderboard spots. While Crew Presence is off, your last 12 hours stay hidden. Crew Presence (which park you're at today and what you're riding) stays off until you turn it on, and it never shows a map pin. A crewmate who logs the seat right next to yours within 10 minutes will see that you rode together. Seat tags, scare ratings and line times from other riders only appear as anonymous totals.

Can I delete my account and my data?

Yes. In the app, go to Profile, then Delete Account. It removes your account and everything tied to it. You can also ask us by email.

What if my home park isn't in Reed Rides yet?

Tell us. Which park comes next is mostly driven by what people ask for.

Do I have to join a crew?

No. Passport, seats, badges, stamps, trivia, Hall of Fame and Ask Reed all work solo. Crew is there if you want it.

Who's behind Reed Rides?

Reed (yes, of Coaster Wars on YouTube) and family, not a theme park company. The app is published by Camps Bay Labs, LLC. If something's broken or missing, tell us. We're the ones who fix it.
